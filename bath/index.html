Bathroom Blueprint

Enter everything in inches (decimals OK, e.g. 57.5). Press Draw to scale. You can also drag fixtures on the sheet.
Room walls (interior measurements)
Start at the top-left corner of the room and go clockwise, one wall at a time. The shape must come back to the start.
On the sheet: drag a wall to move it (a jog is added automatically). Double-click a wall to split it there. Tick Exists for walls that are already built; new walls are hatched and dimensioned with their thickness.

Fixtures
Width = along the wall it backs onto. Depth = how far it sticks out into the room. X / Y = distance from the room's top-left interior corner to the fixture's top-left corner. The towel warmer (P-5) hangs on the wall, so it may sit over other fixtures. The stairs (S-1) float free - put them anywhere, inside or outside the room; the sheet zooms out to include them.
Existing chase
The pipe chase that already runs down to the first bathroom. It starts just outside the right wall, halfway down. Tick the box to show it, untick to hide it. While it is shown, the sheet dimensions it to the top wall and to the bottom wall. Drag the cross on the sheet, or type where its center is (X / Y from the room's top-left interior corner, like the fixtures). The legend on the sheet explains it.
Door
Frosted glass lets light through without showing who is inside.
"Start of wall" is the end you'd reach first going clockwise. For a sliding door, Slides toward is the direction the panel travels as it opens; it needs a clear run of wall as wide as the door that way, and the parked panel is drawn dashed.
Sloped ceiling
Heights you clear, measured out from that wall. The last row is where the slope ends and the ceiling becomes flat.
