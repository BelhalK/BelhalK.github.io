bronze material and procedural arcade-style motion applied at runtime.
The original sculpture has no skeleton or animation clips.

Web asset generated with glTF Transform 4.4.0:
npx @gltf-transform/cli@4.4.0 optimize kobe_bryant/scene.gltf public/assets/kobe-bryant.glb --compress meshopt --simplify-ratio 0.045 --simplify-error 0.005
